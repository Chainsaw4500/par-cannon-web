What the app does

Par Cannon is a golf companion: course maps and distances, scorekeeping, side games, tournaments and friends. It keeps score and tracks who owes whom in the games your group sets up.

No money moves through the app

Par Cannon never holds, sends or collects money, takes no cut of any pot, and doesn't link to payment services. Amounts in the ledger are a record your group keeps for itself. Any money that changes hands happens outside the app, between you and the people you play with.

Age and local laws

Games with money stakes are for players 18 or older. You are responsible for making sure that playing for stakes is legal where you are, and for following your local laws. If it isn't allowed, play for score only.

Distances and scores are estimates

GPS distances, plays-like numbers, course data and maps come from your phone, public map data and third-party providers. They can be wrong. Scores, ledgers and settle-up amounts are only as accurate as what your group enters. Use your own judgment, and follow course rules and staff instructions at all times.
