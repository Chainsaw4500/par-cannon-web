Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This explains what Par Cannon collects, why, and who it's shared with. We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

What we collect

Account: your email address (or Apple sign-in ID), name, username and handicap index, plus your home course if you add one.

Golf: rounds, scores and stats, shots (with where each shot started and finished), games, stakes, side games and settle-up records, and greens you mark on the map.

Social: your friends and friend requests, rounds you post, reactions and comments, tournament chat messages, players you block, and reports you send.

Device: push tokens for notifications and for the lock-screen cards (tee time countdown and live round), with what the card last showed, so we can start, update or end the card on your iPhone. The token for a card is deleted when the card ends; turning the countdown off in Settings deletes the rest.

Location

Your location is used to show distances during a round, to track shots you record, and to find courses near you. It is only used while you are playing a round or searching for courses, and, if you have allowed location, to show the weather on Home (your position is rounded to about 1 km and sent to Open-Meteo). On iPhone, while a round is open and you are on the course, location keeps running when your phone is locked so the lock-screen card can show your distance to the green; it stops when the round ends, and you can turn the card off in Settings. Shot positions you save are stored with your round; your live position is not stored.

Who can see it

People in a round with you see that round's scores, games and ledger. While you play, your friends can follow the round's scores hole by hole (never games or money); you can turn this off in round setup. Rounds you post (scores only), and your reactions and comments, are seen by your friends. Tournament chat is seen by the tournament's players (team chat by your team). Other signed-in users can find your username, name and handicap so they can add you as a friend. Greens you mark are shared with everyone playing that course. Reports you send are read only by the developer.

Services we use

Supabase (database, sign-in and storage, hosted in the US), Brevo (sign-in emails), Apple (Sign in with Apple and app delivery), OpenStreetMap (course layouts and nearby courses, requested from your phone with the map area), Open-Meteo (wind and elevation for the course location, and the weather near you on Home), GolfCourseAPI (course scorecards, requested by our server), and Sentry (crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an error it sends technical details such as the error, app version, device model and iOS version; never your name, email, location or scores). They get only what they need to do that job.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can edit your profile any time (Profile › Edit). To delete your account, go to Settings › Delete account: your profile, friends, posts, comments, chat messages and settings are deleted. Your scores in other players' rounds and tournaments stay, shown as "Former player", so their games still add up. Rounds and tournaments you started pass to another player in them, or are deleted if nobody else has the app.

Children

Par Cannon isn't meant for children under 13, and money-stake games are for adults 18+.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top. Questions or deletion requests: email support@parcannon.com. You can also delete your account yourself in Settings › Delete account.
