Support

Questions, a bug, or a course that looks wrong? Email support@parcannon.com and we’ll get back to you. In the app you can also report a post or a player.

Common questions

Does Par Cannon handle money?

No. Par Cannon keeps score only. It never holds, sends or receives money and doesn't link to payment services. Any settling up happens between friends, outside the app.

Do my friends need a membership?

No. Friends you invite join your rounds and tournaments for free: they score and play the games on their own phone. To start their own rounds, use GPS on their own or keep stats, they can try it free for 30 days.

How do I cancel my membership?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Par Cannon › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period so it doesn’t renew.

Which courses work?

Scorecards for most courses come from our course data; hole maps come from OpenStreetMap. If a course isn’t mapped yet, you can mark the greens yourself.

Why does the app use my location when my phone is locked?

So the lock-screen card keeps your distance to the green up to date during a round. It stops when the round ends. Turn on Battery saver in Settings to use GPS only while the app is open.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete account. Your account and your data are removed.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Par Cannon is on iPhone first; Android comes later.
